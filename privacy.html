Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This privacy policy applies to the mobile games published by Bartpol Games (the "Games") on the Google Play Store, currently including:

  • Survivor Cats
  • Auto Cat Lurkers
  • and any future games released by this developer.

This policy explains how we handle your information when you use any of our Games.

Summary

Our Games themselves do not collect or store any personally identifiable information. However, they use third-party advertising services (Google AdMob) that may collect certain data to serve ads.

Data collected by advertising partners

Our Games use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob (operated by Google LLC) may collect and process data such as:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID / GAID)
  • Device and usage information
  • Approximate location (for ad personalization, if consent is given)
  • Other data described in Google's privacy policy

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Advertising and personalized ads

Our Games may display personalized ads. In the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland, Google may require user consent (under GDPR) before showing personalized ads. Where required, a consent mechanism will be shown before personalized advertising is served.

Local data stored on your device

Our Games store certain data locally on your device (such as game progress, in-game currency, and settings) in the device's local storage. This data is not transmitted to us.

Children's privacy

Our Games are not directed at children under the age required by applicable law (e.g., under 13 in the US). Advertising content rating is set accordingly, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

Our Games request internet access solely to load advertisements and game resources. No other sensitive permissions are required or used.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
